01 — sabse pehle

Haan, meri galti thi.

Jaanu, sabse pehle main bina kisi “lekin”, “par”, “aisa hua kyunki…” ke sirf itna bolna chahta hoon — I'm sorry. Agar meri kisi baat, mere behaviour, meri silence, meri jaldbazi ya mere reaction ne tumhe hurt kiya, toh mujhe uska sach mein afsos hai.

Kabhi-kabhi hum jis insaan ko sabse zyada apna samajhte hain, usi ke feelings ko unknowingly lightly le lete hain. Shayad us moment mein mujhe laga ho ki baat chhoti hai, ya main apni side samjha raha hoon, lekin tumne jo feel kiya woh tumhare liye real tha. Aur mujhe tumhare feeling ko judge karne ke bajaye samajhna chahiye tha.

Main past ko rewind nahi kar sakta. Jo words nikal gaye unhe hawa se wapas nahi la sakta. Lekin main ye choose kar sakta hoon ki aage un words aur actions ke peeche kitni care hogi.

09 — private letter

Ek letter jo main delete nahi karna chahta.

Dear Jaanu,

Shayad tumhe meri apology ke liye perfect words ki zarurat nahi. Shayad tum bas ye feel karna chahti ho ki jis insaan ne tumhe hurt kiya, usse genuinely samajh aaya ki hua kya. Main wahi samajhne ki koshish kar raha hoon.

Mujhe tumhari woh saari little things yaad hain jo relationship ko special banati hain — random baatein, chhoti si nok-jhok, hasi, care, attention, woh moments jahan kuch extraordinary nahi hota phir bhi sab kuch special lagta hai. Main nahi chahta ki meri ek galti in sab achhi cheezon ke upar heavy ho jaaye.

Isliye main tumse “please abhi forgive kar do” nahi keh raha. Main bas keh raha hoon: mujhe chance do ki main apne behaviour se dikha sakun ki ye sorry sirf ek paragraph nahi tha.

Agar tum naraz ho, I understand. Agar tumhe time chahiye, I understand. Agar tum baat karna chahti ho, main sununga. Aur agar tum bas thodi der chup rehna chahti ho, tab bhi main tumhari space respect karunga.

I'm sorry, meri jaan. Dil se. ❤️

Seal par tap karo.
